The Art

Navigation, loss, and recovered legend.

The Nuestra Senora de Atocha was part of Spain's 1622 treasure fleet, remembered for the silver, gold, emeralds, and human stories carried west before disaster struck in the Florida Keys.

Portulano frames that history through a compass rose: not a map of conquest, but a mark of bearing, return, and the enduring romance of ships whose names outlive their voyages.
