Nuestra Senora de Atocha | 1622

Gold, salt air, and a name that survived the wreck.

The Atocha belonged to the Spanish treasure fleet, a world of chart rooms, royal cargo, guarded routes, and dangerous water. The shirt leans into that mythology with La Habana, Canal de Bahama, Los Martires, Tierra Firme, compass bearings, and galleon linework.

Portulano treats the story as luxury travel through time: parchment tones, deep maritime blue, baroque ornament, and a stern view made to feel discovered rather than manufactured.
